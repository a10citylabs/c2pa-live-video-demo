About this demo

Content: samples/live-streaming/Video/Per-segment-C2PA-Manifest-Box-method — 20 fMP4 segments, each carrying its own C2PA manifest. Verification per C2PA-Live-Segment-Validation.md: AES-128-CBC continuity token over each segment's moof+mdat with an IV chained from the anchor segment. Each segment is also checked against a C2PA-spec Merkle tree (Merkle-Proof-Video-Segment-Validation.md, C2PA 2.4 §9.2.3 / Appendix A.5.4): its leaf hash is walked up through a per-segment proof and compared against the root in video_merkle_manifest.json.
