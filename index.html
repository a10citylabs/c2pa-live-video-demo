About this demo

                    

                        Content: samples/live-streaming/Video/Per-segment-C2PA-Manifest-Box-method —
                        20 fMP4 segments, each carrying its own C2PA manifest.
                        Verification per C2PA-Live-Segment-Validation.md:
                        AES-128-CBC continuity token over each segment's moof+mdat
                        with an IV chained from the anchor segment.
